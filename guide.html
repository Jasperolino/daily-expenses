Step by step

Get started in 3 minutes

Install it on your phone

Android: open the app in Chrome → tap ⋮ → Install app.
iPhone: open it in Safari → tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then always open it from the home-screen icon.

Turn on notifications

Go to Settings → Daily reminder → Enable notifications and tap Allow. Without this, the app can't remind you.

Create your spreadsheet (recommended)

In Settings → Google Sheets backup, type your name and Gmail, then tap Create my spreadsheet. Your own sheet is made automatically and shared with your Gmail.

Set a monthly budget (optional)

In Settings → Budget, enter how much you want to spend per month, e.g. ₱15,000.

How the reminders work

If you've already logged something today (or tapped “I spent nothing today”), you won't be bothered. If not, here's what happens:

During the day
Log as you go

The best habit: add each expense right after you pay. It takes a few seconds.

8:00 PM
Friendly reminder

A notification asks what you spent today, and a yellow banner appears in the app.

📝 How much did you spend today?Take 30 seconds to log today's expenses before you forget.
8:00 PM · by email
Email reminder

If you gave your Gmail when creating your spreadsheet, you also get an email — handy on iPhone.

11:00 PM
Final warning

A stronger notification that stays on screen, and a red banner: your streak is about to reset.

⚠️ Don't lose today's expenses!You haven't logged anything today. Unrecorded spending is the #1 reason budgets fail.
Next day
Missed-day warning

When you open the app, a pop-up shows which days are missing and explains why they matter, with one-tap buttons to fill them in or mark them as no-spend days.

Anytime
Change the times

Prefer 9 PM? Set your own reminder and warning times in Settings → Daily reminder.

📱 Phone tip: on Android, reminders arrive even when the app is closed (after installing it). On iPhone, open the app from the home-screen icon and keep the email reminder on for the most reliable alerts.

Build the habit

Streaks 🔥 and no-spend days

The flame at the top of the app counts how many days in a row you've logged. A day counts when you've added at least one expense or tapped I spent nothing today.

Miss a day and the streak starts over — but you can still save it: fill in the missing day from the warning pop-up or from History, and the streak comes back.

Backup & sync

Your own Google spreadsheet

When you tap Create my spreadsheet, a sheet just for you is created. Every expense you add, edit or delete is copied there within seconds. No internet? The app saves on your phone and uploads later automatically — look for ☁️ Synced at the top.

Three tabs, ready for you

Summary — this month by category, totals for every month, and spending by payment method. Expenses — every entry. No-spend days — days you spent nothing.

Use it on your phone and computer

In Settings you'll find your sync code. On another device, open the app, go to Settings → Google Sheets backup → “Already have a spreadsheet on another device?”, paste the code, and the same data appears there.

🔐 Keep your sync code private — anyone with it can see and change your expenses. Your spreadsheet is stored in the app owner's Google Drive and shared with your Gmail.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no payments.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, inside the app. If you create a spreadsheet, a copy is also saved in Google Sheets (in the app owner's Google Drive, shared with your Gmail). Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check that you tapped Enable notifications in Settings and allowed them. On iPhone, notifications only work when the app is added to the Home Screen and opened from there. Also check your phone isn't in Do Not Disturb or battery-saver mode. Use Send a test notification in Settings to check.

I forgot to log yesterday. Can I still add it?

Yes. Change the date above the category list before tapping Add expense, or tap Log this day in History or in the missed-day pop-up.

Can I edit my spreadsheet directly?

Yes. Changes you make in the Expenses tab come back into the app the next time it syncs. Don't delete the hidden first column (ID) — it keeps the app and the sheet matched.

I got a new phone. How do I get my data back?

Install the app on the new phone, go to Settings → Google Sheets backup → “Already have a spreadsheet on another device?”, and enter your sync code. Tip: save your sync code somewhere safe, e.g. in your notes.

It says “Sync failed”.

Usually it's just no internet. Your entries are safe on your phone and will upload automatically when you're back online. You can also tap Sync now in Settings.

How do I stop the email reminders?

Settings → Google Sheets backup → untick “Email me at 8 PM if I haven't logged that day”.
